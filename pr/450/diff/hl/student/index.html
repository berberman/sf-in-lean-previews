830+--  These are *conditional* `simp` lemmas — the equation in the conclusion
831+--  depends on one or more hypotheses. These hypotheses are themselves
832+--  about `Aexp.foldConstants`, which `simp` would have to recursively
833+--  simplify. But the hypotheses say things like "`a₁.foldConstants` equal
834+--  to a numeral" for an abstract `a₁`, and `simp` cannot further simplify
835+--  that. It's a specific case a proof must consider.
836+--
837+--  The `Aexp.foldConstants_cases` theorem helps with that, capturing a
838+--  common pattern of compound expressions: for any `a₁` and `a₂`, either
839+--  folding turns both of them into numerals — in which case folding
840+--  `a₁ + a₂` (and `-`, and `*`) collapses to a single numeral — or at
841+--  least one of them doesn't, in which case folding `a₁ + a₂` (etc.) just
842+--  rebuilds the operator around the already-folded operands. This theorem
843+--  is meant to be consumed with `cases` to split a proof into those two
844+--  scenarios; we will see its use when proving `Aexp.foldConstants_sound`
845+--  below.
