1010+--  These are *conditional* `simp` lemmas — the equation in the conclusion
1011+--  depends on one or more hypotheses. These hypotheses are themselves
1012+--  about `Aexp.foldConstants`, which `simp` would have to recursively
1013+--  simplify. But the hypotheses say things like "`a₁.foldConstants` equal
1014+--  to a numeral" for an abstract `a₁`, and `simp` cannot further simplify
1015+--  that. It's a specific case a proof must consider.
1016+--
1017+--  The `Aexp.foldConstants_cases` theorem helps with that, capturing a
1018+--  common pattern of compound expressions: for any `a₁` and `a₂`, either
1019+--  folding turns both of them into numerals — in which case folding
1020+--  `a₁ + a₂` (and `-`, and `*`) collapses to a single numeral — or at
1021+--  least one of them doesn't, in which case folding `a₁ + a₂` (etc.) just
1022+--  rebuilds the operator around the already-folded operands. This theorem
1023+--  is meant to be consumed with `cases` to split a proof into those two
1024+--  scenarios; we will see its use when proving `Aexp.foldConstants_sound`
1025+--  below.
